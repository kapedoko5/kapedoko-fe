# Event details

## Scope and visitor mode

Operate. A nearby community event listing: poster, when, where, organizer, pin. Job is to decide whether to go, then navigate — not to browse a festival microsite.

## Audience, job, action, constraints

Someone already on Home or Map in the Philippines who saw a nearby gathering. They confirm time and place, optionally call the organizer, and leave with directions. Inherit the bag-label world. Teal event pin, not the brown cafe cup. No WiFi or outlet stamps. No new typeface.

## Direction and memorable moment

Cafe detail as topology: back, stock frame, facts as icon plus words, inset pin map, orange Navigate. The memorable moment is the poster on matte stock and a teal pin on the neighborhood slip.

## Unresolved decisions

None. Home rail and Map pins are local extensions of those surfaces.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A bag-label listing for a nearby gathering — poster, when, where, who — refusing a festival landing page and cafe amenity stamps.

OWN-WORLD: Cool shelf #F2F2F2, matte stock #FAF8F5, ink, teal chrome #1F6F6B, orange Navigate. Kumbh Sans. 16px stock frames. Teal calendar pin vs brown cafe cup.

STORY: The visitor reads when and where, sees the pin, and navigates or calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Back on stock. Poster in a 16px stock frame. Event name. When / Where / Organizer as icon-plus-words. Orange Navigate. Teal pin map under the address.

FORM: Local extension of cafe detail; inherit world; concept-seed skipped (specified inherit). Code-led.

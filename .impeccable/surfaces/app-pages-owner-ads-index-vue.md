## Scope and visitor mode

Operate. Owner-desk Ads hangs one in-cafe poster on cafes the owner manages. Featured photos (max 8) upload inside Edit Cafe Details. Cafe detail shows the live poster and the gallery. Visitor mode is Operate.

## Audience, job, action, constraints

Verified cafe owners, often on a laptop, sometimes a phone. Post one poster, choose one owned cafe or several, replace any live poster on those cafes. Keep hours and listing status out of this page. Bag-label owner desk stays. Home banners stay admin-only.

## Direction and memorable moment

The poster is a print plate you hang on a bag, not a campaign scheduler. Ticking cafes reprints which bags will take the plate. Take down is a quiet stamp off the bag.

## Unresolved decisions

None. Posters live on cafe detail only. Ads is its own owner nav page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Ads is a poster plate you hang on one or more of your cafe bags, refusing a Home-banner scheduler, a hero metric, and a SaaS campaign table.

OWN-WORLD: Shelf #F2F2F2, stock #FAF8F5, ink #1c1917, teal chrome, orange action fills. Kumbh Sans. 16px label corners, hairline borders, no lift shadows.

STORY: The owner uploads one poster, ticks the cafes that should hang it, posts, and can take it down. Visitors see that poster on the cafe page, then featured photos.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Owner menu with Ads active. Heading Ads, lede, stock poster upload, cafe ticks, orange Post poster. Live posters as bag labels with the plate and Take down.

FORM: Owner-desk extension, code-led. Signature interaction: ticking cafes reprints which bags receive the plate; posting stamps it on; Take down clips it off.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

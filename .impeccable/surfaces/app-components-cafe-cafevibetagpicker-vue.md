# Cafe vibe stamp picker

## Scope and visitor mode

Operate. Shared picker on owner and admin cafe edit. Job is to ink up to five catalog stamps onto a listing.

## Audience, job, action, constraints

Verified cafe owners and admins editing a shop. Pick, unpick, see N of 5, stop at five. Disabled stamps still count but stay off the sheet. Empty catalog tells owners to wait for an admin. Not a search list. Not a filter.

## Direction and memorable moment

A stamp pad of tilted bag marks. Pressing one inks it (catalog color wash, inset). Cap mutes the rest.

## Unresolved decisions

None. Public display stays on the bag and cafe detail, not here.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Inking stamps on the bag, refusing checkboxes, pills, and a searchable chip input.

OWN-WORLD: Matte stock face, 8px stamp corners, catalog color on fill and border, ink type, 220ms stamp press. Reduced motion drops tilt and scale, keeps fill.

STORY: Owner taps Quiet and Lively, sees 2 of 5, saves, and those stamps print on the cafe bag.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Heading Vibe stamps, count on the right, one-line lede, wrap of stamps. Empty: “No vibe tags yet. Ask an admin to add some.”

FORM: Local extension of cafe edit; code-led. Signature interaction: stamp press.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

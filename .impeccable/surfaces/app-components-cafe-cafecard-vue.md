# Cafe vibe stamps on bag and detail

## Scope and visitor mode

Operate. Display-only stamps on the cafe bag and cafe detail, after WiFi / Outlets / Pay. Zero tags add no chrome. Not a Home or Search filter.

## Audience, job, action, constraints

Deskless workers scanning Home, Search, Saved, Map, and cafe detail. Read vibe as extra print, then keep choosing by WiFi, plugs, and hours. Icon plus words. No pills. No badge-chip reuse.

## Direction and memorable moment

The same tilted stamp language. On the bag and on detail, vibe sits in its own small carousel under WiFi / Outlets / Pay.

## Unresolved decisions

None. Filters stay work-readiness only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Vibe is printed on the bag, never used to narrow the shelf.

OWN-WORLD: Matte stock, 8px stamp radius, catalog hex on vibe fill and border, ink label, slight tilt. Detail presses drop the verification seal.

STORY: Someone scanning bags sees Quiet under WiFi Yes and knows the room without treating vibe as a filter.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Amenity/pay stamps, then a small vibe carousel (`bag__vibes` / `cafe-detail__vibes`). Empty array: no extra stamps.

FORM: Local extension of the bag label; code-led.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
